🎮 Game Controls & Features

🛣️ Straight Highway with Balanced Speeds: All racers run at identical, balanced paces so anyone can win! Slipstreaming behind racers lets you draft and overtake!

📊 Speedometer Display: Watch your live running speed on the small dial gauge meter in the bottom-right corner!

🗺️ Side Radar Map: Check the vertical radar on the left side to see exactly how far ahead or behind other players are from you!

⏱️ Timer Options: 2, 5, 10, or custom minutes. When the timer hits 0:00, whoever ran the furthest wins!

📱 Dedicated Smartphone & Touch Options
Ergonomic large footer buttons, auto-run cruise trot, and tactile haptic vibration!
📱 Touch Controls Display Mode AUTO
Choose whether big footer buttons are automatically detected on touch devices, forced always ON (useful for testing on PC), or turned OFF.
🔘 Footer Button Size
Pick larger thumb targets for comfortable, accurate taps on small smartphones or tablets.
🐾 Auto-Run (Cruise Trot)
When enabled, your baby animal automatically trots forward at cruising pace so your thumb doesn't get tired holding the run button during long races! Tap 🐾 RUN to sprint, tap 🔻 BRAKE to slow.
📳 Haptic Touch Vibration
Subtle physical vibration pulse when tapping steering, run, dash, item, or brake buttons (supported smartphones/browsers).
